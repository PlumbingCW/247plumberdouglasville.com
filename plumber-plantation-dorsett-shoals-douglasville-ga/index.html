A home from the early 2000s is now more than twenty years old

A home finished in 2006 is about twenty years old this year, and one finished in 2000 is older. Appliances in it may be the originals or may be replacements from any year since. The only way to know is to read the labels.

How to read the age of your water heater

General information that works for any tank unit.

  1. 1

    Find the rating label. It is on the side of the tank.

  2. 2

    Look for the serial number. Many makers encode the month and year of manufacture in it.

  3. 3

    Check the manual or the maker's website. The decoding method differs by brand.

  4. 4

    Tell the plumber. A known age helps decide between repair and replacement.

If the label shows the tank is original to the house, ask about water heater replacement before it fails on its own schedule. A planned swap costs less stress than a flooded closet.

Everyday fixtures in a lived-in home

Over twenty years, the small parts of a home wear out one at a time. A toilet starts running, a faucet drips, a disposal hums without turning. None of these is an emergency by itself, and each is a short visit when caught early.

  • Toilet repair for running or weak-flushing toilets.
  • Faucet repair for drips at the spout or the base.
  • Garbage disposal repair for a jam or a hum.
  • Water heater repair when the water runs lukewarm.

A water heater swap is plumbing work that can call for a permit, and the inspector applies the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with Georgia amendments. Licensing is separate, and Georgia's Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers handles it. Confirm the issuing office for your address before the work is scheduled.

Tankless and tank units are both common choices in a replacement. Price both, and ask what each needs from the gas line, the electrical panel and the venting before you decide.
