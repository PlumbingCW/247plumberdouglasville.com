When a burst pipe is an emergency, and what to do first

A burst or cracked pipe can release gallons a minute, and every minute adds to the damage. The first thing is the main shutoff valve, usually where the water line enters the house, in a basement, a crawlspace, a garage or an exterior meter box. Turn it fully clockwise.

Then turn off the power to the wet area at the breaker if you can reach it safely, open a faucet at the lowest point to drain the line, and call. Our line is open 24/7, and a licensed plumber can be dispatched at any hour. If water is near outlets or the panel, stay clear and call the electric utility or 911.

Before the plumber arrives

A few quick steps limit the damage and make the repair faster.

Stop the water

Close the main shutoff. If you cannot find it, close the meter valve at the street.

Cut power to the wet area

Use the breaker, and do not stand in water to do it.

Move what you can

Lift rugs, electronics and furniture legs out of the water.

Photograph everything

Take pictures and short video before cleanup for your insurer.

Open a low faucet

Draining the line reduces the leftover flow from the break.

Keep the area clear

Wet floors are slippery and ceilings that hold water can fall.

Why pipes burst in homes with mild winters

Hard freezes are not the only cause, and in this part of Georgia they are not the main one. Pipes burst from corrosion, from age, from water pressure that runs too high and from fittings that fail. Pinhole leaks in older copper turn into splits, and joints on older galvanized and polybutylene lines can give way.

When cold does arrive, it can catch homes off guard. Pipes in garages, crawlspaces and exterior walls are often left unprotected because freezing is uncommon, so a hard freeze finds the weak spot. Letting faucets drip and opening cabinet doors during a freeze are cheap precautions.

Repair or replace: how we decide

A split in an otherwise healthy pipe is a repair: cut out the damaged section and install a new piece with proper fittings. When the same pipe has been leaking in several places, or the material is a type known for failure, we talk about replacing a longer run or the whole system.

We also check water pressure after a burst. If it is above the safe range, the pressure regulator is the next thing to replace, so the same thing does not happen at the next weak joint.

Insurance after a burst pipe

Sudden pipe bursts are often covered for the resulting water damage, though the repair to the pipe itself may not be. Call your insurer promptly, and keep receipts for emergency repairs and temporary measures.

Wet drywall and insulation that stay wet for more than a day or two invite mold. A water mitigation company can dry the structure, and the plumber's report documents the cause.

Our leak detection service helps when the burst is behind a wall and you need to find it, and the water line repair page covers breaks outside the house.

Code points after a burst

Georgia's state minimum plumbing code is the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with Georgia amendments, enforced locally. Repairs to supply piping are expected to use approved materials and fittings, so a temporary fix should be followed by a proper repair.
