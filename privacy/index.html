Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Call (770) 214-4545

This policy explains how Douglasville 24/7 Plumbing handles information when you visit this website, call our line or email us. It is written in plain language and applies to the site at 247plumberdouglasville.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us by phone or email, such as your name, address, phone number and a description of your plumbing problem.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends to the site, such as your device type and the pages you view.
  • Call details, such as the time of a call and the number it came from.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want to be recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

How we use it

  • To answer your call and dispatch a plumber.
  • To confirm your address and the work to be done.
  • To improve our service and train the people who answer the line.
  • To keep the site working and secure.

Sharing

We share the details of your request with the plumber who is sent to help you, because they need your address and the problem. We do not sell your information. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This site is a static site and does not use a contact form or user accounts. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this page.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you or ask us to delete it by emailing contact@247plumberdouglasville.com. We will reply as soon as we can.

Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page.
