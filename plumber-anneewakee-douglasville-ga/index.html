Two eras of homes under one name

The same neighborhood name covers houses built forty or fifty years apart. Their plumbing history can differ a great deal, and so can the repairs already done. Tell dispatch roughly when your home was built and what has been replaced.

General information: neither an older home nor a newer one is guaranteed to have a problem. The condition of the pipes, not the date on the deed, is what matters, and a camera or a pressure check shows it.

Sewer or septic on a larger lot

Bigger lots raise a question a smaller lot rarely does, which is whether the home is on public sewer or a septic system. Both exist in Douglas County, and the county's own permit form lists both. Check your property records, or ask the water and sewer authority.

The answer changes how a slow drain gets handled. A main line on sewer can be cleared with sewer line cleaning and checked with a camera inspection. A home on septic needs a different conversation, so say so when you call.
