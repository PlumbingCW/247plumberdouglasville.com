Sewer backup emergency: what to do first

Sewage in a tub, a shower drain or a basement floor drain is a health issue as well as a plumbing one. Stop running water in the house, keep children and pets away, open a window if there is odor, and call. Our line is open 24/7 and sewer backups are treated as urgent.

Do not use drain cleaner on a main line backup. If the line is broken, chemicals do nothing but add hazards for whoever is working on it.

Signs of a damaged sewer line

  • Several fixtures draining slowly or backing up at once.
  • Gurgling from toilets when a tub or washer drains.
  • Sewage odor in the yard or around a cleanout.
  • A soft, sunken or unusually green patch of lawn along the line.
  • Mice or roaches coming in from drains, which can mean a breach in the pipe.

One slow sink is usually a branch clog and not a sewer line. When the symptoms show up in several places, it is time to look at the main.

How we find the problem: camera first

A sewer camera on a push cable travels the line from a cleanout and shows cracks, roots, offsets, bellies and collapsed sections. The camera's locator tells the plumber how deep and where the problem sits, so digging is limited to what is needed. The sewer inspection page covers the camera process in detail.

Trenchless lining and pipe bursting are options when the pipe's condition and the layout allow them. We explain the limits of each, because lining a collapsed pipe is not possible, and an open dig is sometimes the better long-term fix.

Why Douglas County lines fail

Georgia's Piedmont clay soil swells when wet and shrinks when dry, and that movement stresses pipe joints. Older clay and cast iron lines can crack or separate, and newer PVC lines can be crushed by heavy loads or a poor trench backfill.

Tree roots seek the moisture leaking from a small gap, and once inside they grow. This is the practical payoff of a camera look before you pay for a repair. It tells you whether you have a clog, a root problem or a failing pipe.

What a repair visit includes

Repairs follow the camera findings.

  1. 1

    Stop the backup. If the line is blocked, it is cleared enough to relieve the house first.

  2. 2

    Camera and locate. The break is found and marked on the surface.

  3. 3

    Plan the repair. You see the footage and the options, with a clear price for each.

  4. 4

    Repair or replace. The chosen method is carried out and the line is tested.

  5. 5

    Close out. A final camera look confirms the line is whole.

What affects sewer line repair cost

You get a clear price before work starts. These are the factors.

Depth and lengthA deeper or longer run takes more digging or more liner.
MethodOpen dig, pipe bursting and lining have different cost profiles.
Surface restorationDriveways, sidewalks and landscaping add to the work when a trench crosses them.
PermitsUnderground sewer and water service lines are fee items on Douglas County's residential plumbing permit application, so ask the building department.
Septic or county sewerThe permit form distinguishes county sewer from septic tank, and the repair path depends on which you have.

Insurance coverage for sewer lines varies. Some policies cover sudden backup damage with an endorsement, and many exclude the pipe repair itself. Ask your insurer before work starts.

Code expectations for sewer repairs

Georgia's state minimum plumbing code is the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with Georgia amendments, enforced locally. It sets expectations for drain pipe materials, slope and cleanouts, and a permitted repair is inspected against them.
