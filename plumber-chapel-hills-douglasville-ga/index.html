A wide range of home sizes

A three-bedroom home and a seven-bedroom home put different demands on the same plumbing. Water heater size, the number of bathrooms and the length of the pipe runs all shift with the house. When you call, say how many baths you have and how many people live there.

That context lets the plumber decide whether a water heater repair makes sense or the unit is better replaced. It also helps with faucet repair questions, since a house with many fixtures has many places for a small drip to start.

Outdoor spigots on a course-edge lot

Homes that back onto open space often have outdoor spigots at the back and side of the house. Georgia gets freezing nights now and then, and an exposed spigot or a pipe in an outside wall is where a hard freeze does its damage. This is general information, not a finding about Chapel Hills.

Before a freezing night

Small steps cost little and protect the exposed parts of the system.

Disconnect hoses.

A hose left on a spigot traps water against the fitting.

Cover outdoor spigots.

A simple insulated cover helps.

Open cabinet doors.

Warm air reaches pipes in outside walls.

Know your shutoff.

If a pipe does burst, you can stop the water at once.

If a pipe does burst, the steps for burst pipe repair begin with the main valve. For a wet yard with no clear source, water line repair and leak detection find where the water is going.

A replaced water heater or a rerouted supply line may need a permit. Inspections follow the state minimum code, which is the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with Georgia amendments, and the local building office carries them out. Find out which office covers your street first.

What we leave out

We have not verified when each section of Chapel Hills was built, the current operating status of the golf club, or what pipe materials are in any given home. We leave those questions out rather than guess. The golf club is a landmark in listings, not a claim about its present condition.
