Cost of Plumbing a New House: A Douglasville Price Guide

There is no honest single price for plumbing a new house. There is a list of factors, and this guide walks through each one.

Call (770) 214-4545

By the Douglasville 24/7 Plumbing team · 2026.

Plumbing a new house means installing every drain, vent and supply line from the ground up. The bill depends on how many fixtures there are, how far apart they are, what the foundation is and which materials you choose. Quotes vary widely, and the variation usually traces to scope and not to anyone padding the price.

This guide is a way to read a plumbing bid for a new build in Douglasville, GA. It lists what a bid should include, the factors that move it and the published permit fees that Douglas County lists. For planned work, our plumbing installation page explains how we approach it.

The three stages of new-construction plumbing

From ground to finish

Each stage is a separate visit with a separate inspection in many jurisdictions.

  1. 1

    Underground rough-in. Drains and sewer connections are installed in trenches before the slab is poured or the crawlspace is closed.

  2. 2

    Top-out or rough-in. Supply, drain and vent pipes are run through the framing before insulation and drywall.

  3. 3

    Trim-out or finish. Fixtures, faucets, toilets, the water heater and appliance hookups are installed after finishes.

  4. 4

    Inspections. Each stage is tested and inspected where required.

What moves the price

The factors in a plumbing bid

A fair bid lists these, or explains why one does not apply.

Fixture countEvery toilet, sink, tub, shower, hose bib and appliance connection is a rough-in and a trim.
Number of bathrooms and floorsStacked bathrooms share vents and stacks. Spread-out layouts need more pipe.
Foundation typeSlab, crawlspace and basement each change how drains and supplies are run.
Distance from the streetA longer water and sewer connection adds trenching and pipe.
MaterialsPEX, copper, PVC and cast iron differ in material and labor.
Water heater typeA tank, a tankless unit and a heat pump unit have different needs.
Fixture gradeBuilder-grade and designer fixtures are priced very differently, and some are supplied by the owner.

Permit fees published by Douglas County

Douglas County's 2025 residential plumbing permit application lists a minimum permit fee of 20 dollars and per-item fees. Examples on the form include 2.25 dollars for a sink, lavatory, bathtub or water heater, 2.50 dollars for a water closet, 0.20 dollars per foot for an underground sewer or water service line and 2 dollars per foot for a gas line.

These are the numbers on the form we read, and fees change, so confirm with the county building department before you budget. The point is that permit fees are a small share of the project, and the larger costs are labor, materials and fixtures.

Ask the bidder to explain any line you do not understand. Plumbers who work carefully do not mind questions.

Where owners save money, and where they should not

Owners can save by choosing a compact layout, stacking bathrooms and picking standard fixtures and a tank water heater. They can also save by ordering fixtures early, so the rough-in matches them and no one has to move a pipe.

Where not to save is in the hidden parts. Pipe material, slab drains, vents and the sewer connection are the pieces you cannot easily fix later, and a better pipe costs little in the context of a house.

Timing and coordination

Plumbing sits between the excavator, the framer and the drywall crew, and delays at one stage push the next. Confirm the rough-in dates with the builder so the plumber is not waiting on the slab or the framing, since idle time shows up in the bid.

Order fixtures early. A tub or shower valve that arrives late can hold up an inspection, and a fixture swapped after rough-in may need the stubs moved.

Douglasville: licensing, permits and sewer type

Georgia licenses plumbers through the Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers. The county application asks for the contractor's state card number, so ask who holds the license on your bid. The county building department at 4655 Timber Ridge Drive, Douglasville, handles plumbing permit applications for the county.

The permit form also asks whether the site is on county sewer or a septic tank. That choice changes the underground work, and it is worth settling early. The Douglasville-Douglas County Water and Sewer Authority is the contact for water and sewer service, and homes in Lithia Springs and the towns on our service areas page may use different providers.

Georgia's state minimum plumbing code is the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with Georgia amendments, enforced locally. New construction is inspected against it at each stage, which is why bids should list permits and inspections.
