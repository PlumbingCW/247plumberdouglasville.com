Cast Iron Pipe Lining Cost: What Drives the Price in Douglasville

Lining a cast iron pipe avoids a trench, but the price depends on the pipe, not just the footage. These are the factors that move it.

Call (770) 214-4545

By the Douglasville 24/7 Plumbing team · 2026.

Cast iron drain and sewer pipe was the standard in many houses for decades, and it can last a long time. It also rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually cracks or flakes. Pipe lining, sometimes called cured-in-place pipe, is one way to repair it without digging up the whole run.

Searches for the cost of cast iron pipe lining usually want a single number. A fair answer is a list of factors, because a short accessible run and a long deep one are different jobs. This page explains the factors so you can read a quote with confidence. If your sewer is backing up now, call, because the line is open 24/7.

How lining works

A flexible felt tube soaked in epoxy resin is pulled or inverted into the old pipe. It is inflated against the pipe wall and cured with hot water, steam or light. When it hardens, it forms a new pipe inside the old one, with a smooth surface and no joints.

The process is done from existing cleanouts or small access points, so the amount of digging is limited. Before lining, the pipe is cleaned by cable or hydro jetting, and a camera confirms the pipe is a good candidate.

When lining works and when it does not

Pipe condition and the likely repair

A camera inspection decides this, not the age of the pipe.

The factors that set the price

Comparisons with excavation should include the restoration cost. A trench through a driveway, a patio or landscaping adds cost that lining often avoids. A fair quote lists both methods and the reasons for the recommendation.

Questions to ask a contractor

  • Did you inspect the pipe with a camera, and can I see the footage.
  • How will the pipe be cleaned before lining.
  • How will branch connections be reopened.
  • What is the liner's wall thickness and warranty.
  • Does the quote include a final camera inspection.
  • Who handles the permit, if one is needed.

A contractor who answers these clearly is easier to trust than one who quotes a single price per foot from the sidewalk.

Lining versus replacement over the long run

Lining is a good fit when the host pipe is sound enough to hold the liner in place and the route is hard to reach. Replacement is better when the pipe has failed, has a belly or when the sections are short and open. Plastic replacement pipe is not subject to the rust that took the cast iron, so it resets the clock.

The honest comparison considers the whole property, including trees, hardscape and how long you plan to stay.

Questions that sharpen a lining quote

  • How much of the line is being lined, and where does it start and end?
  • Is there a camera video showing the pipe before and after?
  • How are branch lines and cleanouts handled?
  • What is the warranty and what does it exclude?
  • Does the price include permits and cleanup?

A quote that answers these gives you something to compare. A quote that gives one number and a handshake does not.

Douglasville: permits, sewer type and soil

Douglas County's residential plumbing permit application lists underground sewer lines among its items and prices them by the foot, and it asks whether the property is on county sewer or a septic tank. Ask the Douglas County building department what your lining or replacement job requires before work begins.

Georgia's Piedmont clay shifts as it wets and dries, which is one reason older joints separate. If your line serves a house in Douglasville or Lithia Springs, the camera will show whether the problem is the pipe or the soil around it.

Georgia's state minimum plumbing code is the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with Georgia amendments, enforced locally. A permit and inspection may apply to lining or replacement, so ask the county before the work starts.
