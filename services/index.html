Water Heaters

Water Heater Repair

Leaks, no hot water, pilot trouble and failed elements on gas, electric and hybrid tanks.

Water Heater Replacement

Failed tanks swapped out, with removal of the old unit and a price before work.

Water Heater Installation

New and upgraded installs with sizing, venting, expansion tanks and drain pans.

Tankless Water Heaters

Wall-mounted units installed, repaired, flushed and descaled.

Drains and Sewer

Drain Cleaning

Sinks, tubs, floor drains and main lines opened with cables and cameras.

Hydro Jetting

High-pressure scouring of grease, scale and root growth in main drain lines.

Sewer Line Repair

Cracked, rooted, offset or collapsed lines found by camera and repaired or replaced.

Sewer Line Cleaning

Roots, grease and wipe blockages cleared from main sewer lines.

Sewer Camera Inspection

Video inspection of sewer lines with locating, for buyers, backups and repair planning.

Leaks and Pipes

Leak Detection

Meter running, bills climbing or stains spreading, traced with acoustic and thermal tools.

Slab Leak Repair

Hot or cold line leaks under a concrete slab, found by listening and thermal tools.

Burst Pipe Repair

Split pipe and failed fittings stopped and repaired, with the cause identified.

Water Line Repair

Underground service lines repaired or replaced, and low-pressure causes traced.

Repiping

Galvanized, polybutylene and pinhole-prone copper replaced with new supply lines.

Fixtures and Installs

Toilet Repair

Running, leaking, clogged and wobbly toilets repaired, plus new toilet installs.

Faucet Repair

Dripping faucets, seized shutoffs, shower valves and sink connections repaired.

Garbage Disposal Repair

Jams, hums, leaks and failed disposals diagnosed, repaired or replaced.

Plumbing Installation

New fixtures, remodel rough-ins, outbuilding water lines and gas connections.

Commercial Plumbing

After-hours and scheduled plumbing for shops, restaurants and offices.
